Case File 05: Vault of the Vanished

A protected archive was recovered from a damaged storage system. Several files appear to have disappeared, and the remaining evidence is incomplete.

0xGhost left only two messages:

“If you want in, just ask to be let in. Though nothing stays quite the way you remember it.”

“Deletion is not ….”

Examine the archive, recover what was lost and uncover the flag.
