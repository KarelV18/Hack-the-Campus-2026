Case File 04: Double Vision

During the incident, unusual traffic was detected on the robotics lab network. The capture was secured inside the robotics display area before it could be examined.

Locate the display area and determine the proper way to obtain authorized access. A QR code inside contains the evidence required for your investigation.

Do not enter without permission, follow someone through a secured door, or interfere with laboratory equipment.

Remember: what appears ordinary at first may deserve a second look.
